Why we do this

Berlin is full of people. Making friends here still takes work.

Bars are loud, meetups feel like job interviews, and small talk in a second language is tiring. Many of us arrived for a job or a partner and found the social side harder than the paperwork.

So we sit people at the same table and give them something to make. When your hands are in clay or yarn, the silence is comfortable and the questions come on their own. Same small group, every week, until the faces are familiar.

What to expect

An evening with us

Every workshop follows the same shape, so even the first time feels familiar. You never have to speak to the whole room.

  1. 18:30

    Arrive and settle

    Name tags, a drink, and a host who introduces you to someone at your table.

  2. 18:45

    Round of hellos

    One easy question for everyone. Last week's was the best snack in your home country.

  3. 19:00

    Make something

    The facilitator shows the first step, then works the room while you get going in pairs.

  4. 20:30

    Tea and talk

    Tools down, show what you made, swap numbers if you want to. Nobody rushes you out.
