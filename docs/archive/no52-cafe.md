# No. 52 Cafe (archived)

- Status: **Retired**
- Date retired: 2026-09-14
- Reason: the cafe is closing. Guidance is kept here rather than deleted because
  the mark may still appear on signage and printed material during wind-down.
- Decision record: [`docs/decisions/002-retire-no52-cafe.md`](../decisions/002-retire-no52-cafe.md)

Nothing in this file is current design-system guidance. Do not apply it to new
work. It is a record of what Brand Book v1.0 specified, so existing signage and
print runs can still be interpreted and matched.

## Naming

Source: Brand Book v1.0 p.6.

Always written **No. 52 Cafe**, or **No. 52** for short. "Cafe" is spelled
**without an accent**, and there is a space between "No." and "52".

Incorrect forms: `No.52 Café` · `No52` · `No.52` · `Pavillon Café` ·
`KARUNA Pavillon`.

## Logo

Source: Brand Book v1.0 p.15. The cafe had its own mark, separate from the
ForEveryone logo system.

- **Primary:** the `No. 52` wordmark, for cafe signage, menus, and social media.
- **Secondary (compact):** `52` set inside the circular `o`, for small or square
  formats (favicons, profile pictures). The wordmark was used wherever it fit;
  the compact mark only when it did not.
- **Clear space:** X = **half the height of the `N`** in `No. 52`, on all sides.
- **Minimum sizes:** primary 32 px / 8 mm; secondary 40 px / 10 mm.
- **Colour:** Charcoal on light backgrounds (Lime, Soft Lavender, Warm White);
  White on Blue or photographic overlays.
- The ForEveryone background-combination and incorrect-usage rules in
  [`logo-usage.md`](../logo-usage.md) applied equally to this mark.

### At a glance

| | No. 52 Cafe |
|---|---|
| Primary format | `No. 52` wordmark |
| Secondary | `52` inside the `o` (compact) |
| Min digital | 32px (primary) / 40px (secondary) |
| Min print | 8mm (primary) / 10mm (secondary) |
| Approved colours | Charcoal, White |
| Safe zone | ½ height of N, all sides |

### Asset status

`prototype/public/images/logo/no52-wordmark.svg` and `no52-compact.svg` were
documented in `logo-usage.md` and `ASSETS.md` as in-repo redraws traced from
Brand Book v1.0 p.15, pending official vectors from the brand team. **Neither
file was ever present in the repository.** `prototype/public/images/logo/` has
only ever held the four ForEveryone marks. Anyone looking for the No. 52 vectors
should go to the Canva Brand Kit or Google Drive, not to this repo.

## Cafe signs (print)

Source: Brand Book v1.0 p.38.

- Keep signage simple, clear, playful, and welcoming.
- Make information easy to read at a glance: clean layout, strong hierarchy,
  minimal clutter.
- Place the ForEveryone and No. 52 logos where they do not distract from the
  main message.
- Confirm the print method and CMYK values with Roxana before starting.
